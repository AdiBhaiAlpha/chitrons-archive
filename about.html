About

I'm Chitron Bhattacharjee — a developer, programmer and writer based in Bangladesh. I work across artificial intelligence, web development, bot development, and interface design, combining technical work with creative interests.

Technology for me isn't just an academic subject — it's something I build with, experiment with, and think about constantly. Most of my projects sit at the intersection of software, AI, automation and design.

Alongside technology, I write poetry, stories and reflective pieces, using writing as another way to explore people, society, ideas and emotion.

What I Do

I work across several areas of software development and creative technology:

  • AI Development — building conversational AI systems, working with large language models, and creating AI-powered applications
  • Web Development — full-stack development using JavaScript, Node.js, HTML, CSS and modern web technologies
  • Bot Development — creating chatbots and messaging bots with Node.js, API integrations and automation
  • UI/UX Design — designing clean, minimal, human-centered digital interfaces
  • Cybersecurity — exploring digital security and related technologies
  • Writing — poetry, stories, essays and reflective writing

Philosophy

I believe in learning by building. Most of what I know comes from experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and building something slightly better each time. I'm drawn to technology that is useful and human-centered — not just technically impressive, but genuinely helpful.

I'm interested in combining creativity and technology, and in creating things rather than only consuming them.
